Indras Net · Hexagonal Manifold + Full-Circle Harmonic Table

Two independent play states: Immersive · Hexagonal Manifold retains the concentric 37-pad octave instrument. Harmonic Table · Tonnetz adds a full-circle, edge-to-edge isomorphic hex grid (every pad lit), with vertical fifths (+7), up-right major thirds (+4), up-left minor thirds (+3), absolute MIDI pitches and scale-based colours. Traditional Tonnetz stays static; Sound Reactive has distinct spectrum-driven rings. Saved sounds lets players save, reload, export and import their own sound configurations.
